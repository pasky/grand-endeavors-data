## All three: label what is fitted and what is structural
Wherever a mapping rests on a structural choice (rung spacing, the effort of one example, a weighting
of analogues), present it as a labelled scenario range with its sensitivity. Do not call it a fitted
interval. Reserve "fitted" or "measured" for quantities estimated from two or more ledger points.

## milestone:the-linux-kernel-patch (major)
1. The assisted ladder is already above the size threshold: substantially AI-written contributions of
   more than 10k lines have been merged into major projects (Ladybird, Node.js; Bun far larger), with a
   human submitting. The remaining gap is the AI as **submitter**: it writes and shepherds the patch
   through review on its own. State this crux first.
2. Equating the required 80% horizon with the human effort of the whole contribution is the
   *definition* of a task horizon (×1), not a calibrated transfer. Say so. Replace the single-example
   effort (one Ladybird estimate) with a scenario range for the contribution's effort, citing every
   ledger point that bears on it. Add a messiness scenario, because real review-gated work is harder
   than METR-style tasks. Use the optimistic bound from the oracle-checked benchmarks (the gap between
   assisted rungs reached at about 1.7–3.8 h of horizon and the effort they embody) as a quantified
   lower scenario.
3. The review lag rests on n = 0 qualifying cases. Either cite a reference class with counts from the
   ledger, or keep it explicitly widened and labelled assumed.

2. The workshop rung (2025-03) predates the blended series, so its horizon is a backcast. Propagate its
