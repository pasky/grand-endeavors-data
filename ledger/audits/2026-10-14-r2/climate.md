## Findings

**MILESTONE — MAJOR: Balance’s principal probability reduction is falsely attributed to a physical-consistency correction.** The change_note attributes .188→.091 and p10 2093→null to requiring CO₂ and non-CO₂ separately to reach assumed residual floors. Net-zero all-GHG does not require that: removals can offset emissions above either floor. For example, gross CO₂=4, non-CO₂=3.5 and removal=7.5 gives net GHG=0 and net CO₂=−3.5, but the new test rejects it when Rc=2.5. The old apparent ordering problem also reflects Ceiling’s artificial removal cap, `min(Rc,C)`, not physical CO₂ neutrality. The models still use removals differently: Balance offsets both residuals, while Ceiling prevents net-negative CO₂. Component fitting is a legitimate audit correction; this additional restriction is not a demonstrated error fix. Use consistent net flows or explicitly justify the added deployment/abatement restriction; do not describe its roughly ten-percentage-point effect as physically necessary. This fails rule 7’s truthful attribution requirement.

**MILESTONE — MAJOR: Ceiling still substitutes CO₂ neutrality for the chosen physical-temperature-peak quantity.** Round 1 explicitly warned that neutrality neither dates the temperature maximum nor excludes subsequent non-CO₂ warming. The new model again dates success at CO₂ neutrality, asserting that approximately zero CO₂ zero-emissions commitment establishes this equality. Its non-CO₂ budget perturbation is independent of the actual non-CO₂ trajectory. Re-running the disclosed m=0 sensitivity leaves Ceiling exactly .0392 despite removing non-CO₂ deceleration entirely. AR6 SPM D.1 requires strong reductions in other greenhouse gases; Table SPM.2 footnote c conditions budgets on SR1.5 non-CO₂ pathways. Those conditions are not reconciled with these draws. Source: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/ . This is a remaining quantity/mapping defect, distinct from the owner’s recognition definition. Model the chosen physical peak, or label and test the neutrality-to-peak mapping as an assumption rather than a sourced identity.
