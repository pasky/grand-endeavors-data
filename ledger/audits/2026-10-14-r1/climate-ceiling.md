## Findings

1. **DEFINITION — MAJOR: achievement is not operationally defined.** The intended quantity is correctly peak long-term global warming, not present temperature, an annual anomaly, or cooling after overshoot. But the framework does not specify baseline, averaging window, persistence or how a future maximum becomes an achieved event. The implementation substitutes CO₂ neutrality by 2100 plus a temperature-budget test; neutrality neither establishes the temperature-peak date nor excludes later non-CO₂ warming. **Fix:** approve a temperature/verification definition and forecast its actual completion event; distinguish physical peak, recognition and permanently avoiding overshoot.
