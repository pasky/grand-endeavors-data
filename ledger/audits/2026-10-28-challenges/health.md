# Health challenge audit — 2026-10-28

Scope: RUBRIC §§6–7, literal framework definitions, all five assessments, all 99 distinct cited events (claims, dates, publication dates, verification and source metadata), and a ledger-wide search for omitted negative results. This is a ledger audit, not a fresh external verification of every paper. Corrected effective events are not treated as unverified. All five labels meet the 100-character limit (97/93/97/100/100); prospects contain no event IDs, and their dated plans are sourced, not assessor forecasts.

## health challenge:brain-preservation

**MINOR — substantive basis needs repair; green is not conclusively wrong.** `2026-03-06-japan-first-ipsc-derived-therapies-approved` establishes conditional approval for Parkinson's **motor symptoms**, not restoration of cognition/identity. `2026-03-25-fda-approves-avlayah-first-bbb-shuttle` establishes brain delivery and a biochemical response in Hunter syndrome, not cognitive repair. Both have regulatory corroboration, but neither alone demonstrates the framework's “repair at scale.” Remove their unqualified treatment as substantive cognitive-repair results. However, `2026-02-10-epfl-engram-osk-partial-reprogramming` reports peer-reviewed restoration of learning/memory in aged and Alzheimer's-model mice: a directly relevant candidate substantive precursor. “Animal only” does not itself disqualify a substantive advance under §7. Reassess that result explicitly; green can be retained on a justified new-capability reading, otherwise yellow **Inching** (subject to the setback weighing below). Do not downgrade merely because human repair is not achieved.

**MINOR — omitted setbacks make the weighing incomplete.** Add `2025-11-24-oral-semaglutide-fails-evoke-alzheimers-rv` and `2025-11-21-jnj-posdinemab-fails-pivotal-trial-rv`: both are effective, non-legacy failures inside the window despite lacking challenge tags. Consider `2026-03-25-anavex-withdraws-blarcamesine-ema` (EMA found effectiveness unproven), and list the missed CELIA primary endpoint from `2026-05-14-diranersen-celia-phase-2-topline`, currently only acknowledged within an advance. Explicitly weigh these against cognitive restoration, not against unrelated approvals. They do not automatically compel red, but “neither reverses the approvals” is not the required comparison.

**MINOR — wording.** Narrow Avlayah's “first” to **first FDA-approved** BBB-engineered biologic; that is what its source establishes. The basis's 91% reduction among 44 measured patients is supported by the verification note (47 enrolled), so this is not a numerical error. Shorten the long rationale and explain “BLA,” “OLE” and other technical terms in prospects or omit them.

## health challenge:causal-model-of-aging

**MINOR; retain green as a defensible judgment.** `2026-06-12-hesjas-dna-hypermethylation-causes-aging-pathology` is not merely a disease biomarker: the verified Nature Genetics result experimentally links age-like methylation changes to stem-cell dysfunction and several age-related pathologies in humans and mice. Treating this causal-versus-correlative resolution as a substantive contribution toward the unified model is reasonable; §7 does not require the advance itself to deliver the complete model. The assessment correctly says prediction of safe interventions remains unresolved. The mortality clocks (`2026-05-27-universal-transcriptomic-hallmarks-mortality-clocks`) are appropriately not promoted to a causal model or a substantive predictive gain.

**MINOR — qualify the rapamycin claim.** For `2026-04-15-rapa-ex-01-sirolimus-blunts-exercise-gains`, the primary analysis was not significant (p=0.089); blunting was significant only per protocol (p=0.007). Say “did not improve exercise gains and may have reduced them,” rather than presenting impairment as an unqualified trial conclusion. The basis discloses this distinction, but readers should not need to unfold it. ITP's eleven failures and this tradeoff are included; judging them not to outweigh a causal discovery is reasonable. Prospects are sourced, though the dog-drug approval detour is less useful than the proposed human multi-disease test.

## health challenge:predictive-biomarkers

**OK — retain green.** `2026-07-15-jama-plasma-ptau217-predicts-cognitive-impairment` plus `2026-02-19-plasma-ptau217-clock-predicts-alzheimers-onset` provide peer-reviewed, multi-cohort evidence directly on presymptomatic molecular prediction. Calling that substantive validation is defensible, not a requirement that population screening already be solved. The 2,684 participants, 38% five-year and approximately 78% ten-year risks match the ledger. Individual prognosis and population specificity remain limitations and are disclosed. The ALS 1.6-year error and Galleri figures also match.

The major negative, `2026-09-22-nejm-nhs-galleri-primary-results`, is included accurately (stage III–IV IRR 1.03; stage IV 0.86, upper confidence bound 1.00). Giving it less weight than advances across other disease classes is a disclosed reasonable judgment, not a mandatory red. No additional material omission found for molecular detection; failure of Lp(a)-lowering treatment is not failure of a diagnostic signature. Prospects correctly describe a pending decision rather than an approval.

## health challenge:programmable-immunity

**MINOR; retain green.** `2026-06-30-fda-approves-tregzi-first-treg-cell-therapy` supports a substantive tolerance-preserving capability: FDA-reviewed randomized evidence, 78% versus 38.4% one-year chronic-GVHD-free survival. It does not prove decades of preventive immune control, but is directly relevant progress on the tolerance constraint. Conversely, `2026-06-22-nmpa-satri-cel-first-solid-tumor-car-t` treats advanced cancer; approval alone does not establish preventive immune monitoring or sustained tolerance. Move that to contextual capability evidence, not a second unqualified substantive advance on the stated challenge.

**MINOR — safety accounting.** `2026-03-25-eso-t01-in-vivo-bcma-car-t-phase-1` reports grade ≥3 events in all five patients, one death and early stopping, yet appears only among advances. Establish the actual 2025 stopping/result date before counting its harm in this window: the March 2026 publication must not reset the clock. If in-window, represent the safety result in setbacks as well. `2026-06-25-nejm-ten-year-cd19-car-t-lymphoma-outcomes` also warrants discussion of its 21% second-cancer incidence, without attributing those cancers to CAR-T or calling the risk newly increased without evidence. The existing deaths/holds, relapses and vaccine failures are substantial and mostly well covered; retaining green against the Treg result is defensible, not automatically overturned by counting failures. Correct the basis's “one product” shorthand: the cited August event includes both Novartis and BMS products. Prospects are sourced but should replace the acquisition list and acronyms with the next clinical tests.

## health challenge:safe-in-vivo-rejuvenation

**MAJOR 1 — should be yellow Inching on the stated evidence judgments.** The sole substantive advance, `2026-03-06-japan-first-ipsc-derived-therapies-approved`, concerns implantation of cells reprogrammed **outside** the body. Conditional approval corroborates those products, not resetting the recipient's adult cells in vivo. “Regeneration” does not make every stem-cell transplant a rejuvenation result. Remove this substantive flag (and treat the grafts as adjacent context). The remaining advances are explicitly non-substantive: the small peer-reviewed senolytic fibrosis trial (`2026-10-01-dasatinib-quercetin-mash-phase-2-nature-metabolism`; 8/17 versus 1/14 correctly quoted) supports Inching; ER-100 and Rubedo remain company-reported early signals. Do not silently promote these to preserve green. Suggested label: “Inching: senolytics reduced liver scarring; lasting in-body rejuvenation remains unproven”.

**MAJOR 2 — unsupported first-in-human claim in rationale.** Calling ER-100 “the first partial-reprogramming therapy given inside the human body” contradicts the assessment's own `2026-09-30-khl-unregulated-osk-gene-therapy-humans`: it reports an OSK-containing intervention dosed on 11 March, before ER-100's 9 June dosing. Neither report proves rejuvenation. Fix to “the regulated ER-100 trial reported its first three patients,” avoiding an unsupported global first. Likewise, describe the Japanese products precisely as conditional iPSC-derived approvals, not the first stem-cell repair therapies generally.

Setback judgment: `2026-06-04-oskm-liver-failure-oxidative-stress-nac` and `2026-01-29-senolytic-comparison-21-agents-resistance` contain important safety/resistance limitations, but also mechanism/mitigation advances. The disclosed interpretation that these clarify existing barriers rather than constitute net new setbacks is reasonable; no compulsory red established. UNITY's September 2025 closure is outside the window. Prospects name sourced trials and timelines; prune the financing detail and explain the acronyms.
