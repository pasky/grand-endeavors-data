# Open-challenge momentum: audit of the first assessments (2026-10-28)

The first momentum statuses of the 21 open challenges (gather/RUBRIC.md §7, assessed as of
2026-10-28 without an independent review stage) were audited by three deep-mode reviewers,
one report per group of sections: robots.md (robots-software, robots-hardware),
rockets-fusion-climate.md, health.md. Each checked every advance and setback against the
ledger: on the challenge as worded, a step change, a demonstrated result, more than the
proponent's word, in the window; missing setbacks; the numbers in the label and rationale.

Colour findings (MAJOR), checked by the owner's session against the cited events:
- robots-software continuous-learning: green -> yellow (in-session adaptation on ARC-AGI-3 is
  not lasting behaviour change);
- robots-hardware power-efficiency-cost: green -> yellow (the RAIBO2 marathon ran in
  November 2024; the 2026 Nature paper reports it);
- fusion deep-geothermal: green -> yellow (Cape Station's 33 MW rests on Fervo's release only);
- health safe-in-vivo-rejuvenation: green -> yellow (iPSC-derived grafts are reprogrammed
  outside the body).
Factual fixes: dexterous-manipulation (92% across 31 objects), closed-loop-life-support (BLiSS
human testing is planned), aneutronic-fusion (alpha particles, not reactions, a second),
deep-geothermal (well length vs depth), net-zero-transition (GHG excluding land use),
safe-in-vivo-rejuvenation (no first-in-human claim). The minor findings are suggestions.

Mechanism changes from it (commit 55d0817): the assess review stage covers challenges;
§7 dates a result by its claim when that is earlier, seeks setbacks among all of a
section's events, and keeps event ids out of the prospects.
