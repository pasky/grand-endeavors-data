# Astra — independent options

The central problem is **identifiability, not rater consistency**. Votes about transfer are not measurements of work. And 65,000 operating hours are not a lower bound on autonomous productive hours. Cost estimates below are planning estimates, not measured run costs.

## astra-1 — Audited O*NET capability coverage (baseline)
**What:** Dashboard: **≈0.2% of US physical work-time potentially covered**, explicitly experimental, not work automated. Current cross-model estimates are 0.11–0.32%; that is model sensitivity, **not** a confidence interval.

**Why/how:** Retain v5’s frozen decomposition, employment weights, shared catalogue and candidate discovery. Replace voting-as-truth with a named human adjudicator inspecting original evidence for every positive/borderline mapping; audit sampled negatives and retrieval omissions. Publish the decisions. Sample all strata, including E0; v5 currently omits it. Preserve PPS draw multiplicities. Freeze adjudications, and change them only for new evidence or documented corrections.

Next quarter, review new catalogue evidence against the same panel; distinguish new capabilities from newly discovered old evidence. Expand toward 150 tasks only after adjudication establishes construct validity. Budget several setup days, then roughly one reviewer-day/quarter plus agent calls.

**Failure:** Human adjudication still cannot establish transfer that nobody tested; sub-step durations remain invented estimates. Stable judgments can be stably wrong. No defensible pace colour yet.

## astra-2 — Admit the measurement gap; retain milestones
**What:** Dashboard: **“Autonomous GP work: not yet measurable.”** If a numeric progress display is essential, show **0/4 independently qualified GP milestones** provisionally: coffee, Fukushima, Blue Collar Shift, Dark Line; keep robotaxis separate. Confirm the four current assessments before publishing. This is a milestone tally, **not a substitute work-share KPI**.

**Why/how:** Keep the existing verified ledger and milestone assessments; add no synthetic series or pace assessment. Next quarter recount literal achievements and check whether an outside provider has begun publishing an appropriate recurring series. About one hour/quarter incremental maintenance; no new research pipeline.

**Failure:** No smooth number and no measured trend. Equal milestone weights have no scientific meaning, so display the count only as navigation, never “percent of robotics solved.” This explicitly relaxes the one-KPI requirement.

## astra-3 — [novel] Customer acceptance-test sentinel
**What:** Follow one frozen real customer workload rather than extrapolating to the economy: **accepted napkins per 18-hour shift / 1,500 required**, Dyna–Din Tai Fung. Today's reported operational value is **106%**, versus about 32% at launch. **Autonomous value: unavailable** until control/intervention evidence accompanies those exact runs.

**Why/how:** The customer supplies the quality bar and required output; no O*NET transfer, guessed task weights or arbitrary productivity threshold. Obtain quarterly customer-signed output and control logs covering the complete reporting period, including failed runs. Count only accepted output completed without action-level human assistance; disclose human preparation/recovery separately. Initial outreach, then half a day/quarter if the parties cooperate. Publish 106% only as reported operational performance, not as the autonomy KPI.

**Failure:** A cooperative vendor is indispensable; task-specific progress is not generality. The earlier 24-hour no-intervention demo cannot establish autonomy of later restaurant throughput. Without cooperation, abandon this option rather than impute autonomy.

## astra-4 — [novel] Verified software-only redeployment certificates
**What:** Quarterly count of **customer-witnessed transitions of the same physical robot between different productive jobs, by software alone, autonomously at both endpoints**. Today: **0 admitted certificates** in this proposed registry—not a claim that zero transitions exist.

**Why/how:** Directly test the owner's defining proposition instead of inferring generality from humanoid shape. Each certificate identifies the unit, before/after jobs, unchanged hardware, accepted output and control evidence. Deduplicate repeated transitions between the same job pair on the same unit. Seed from catalogue leads; require customer confirmation before admission. Budget several days establishing the protocol and outreach, then half a day/quarter; next quarter count newly admitted certificates.

**Failure:** Primarily measures documented redeployment, not labor volume; easy job variants and disclosure incentives can distort growth. Zero may persist despite progress. Not suitable for pace scoring without further methodological work.

## Ranking
**astra-2 > astra-1 > astra-3 > astra-4.** Prefer astra-2 now: trustworthiness outranks producing a number. If a capability number is non-negotiable, fund the small positive-case audit in astra-1 before scaling inference. Astra-3 is the best bounded experiment for new measurements; astra-4 is the weakest headline.
