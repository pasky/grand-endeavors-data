## Non-author audit: argon-6

The common admitted deployment list supports a small **case register**, not the claimed counting unit.

- **Boundary counterexample:** Ledger L185 gives GXO two activities: AMR→conveyor transfer **and** stacking totes elsewhere. L210 gives Longcheer **more than four workflows**. Argon counts each as one. Conversely GXO, Schaeffler and Galbot could all be one “container transport” workflow. No factual event changed between those counts.
- **SOC does not repair this:** Our mapping experiment already assigned the same physical work to different occupations. Adding SOC to the key can multiply one workflow rather than deduplicate it.
- **“Active” is unsupported:** Earlier dated deployment reports do not establish continued operation this quarter. BMW’s explicit ending is observable; silence about other endings is not continued-operation evidence.
- **History:** L205 dates China Post’s arrival to November 2025 but was published June 2026. The proposed 2025 count therefore mixes retrospective event history with contemporary knowledge unless explicitly backcast. No row-level 1→3→6 derivation was supplied.

Thus I reject argon-6 **as presently specified**, while accepting its premise that named deployment cases are much more reproducible than O*NET assignments.
