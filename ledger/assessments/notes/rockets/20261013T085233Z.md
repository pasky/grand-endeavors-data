## The Deluge — 100,000 t delivered to orbit in a single year

1. **Gap.** Quantity: annual payload mass to Earth orbit (JSR msatannual, collector rows).
   Latest: 3,194.0 t (2025). Target 100,000 t/yr. Gap 100000/3194.0 = 31.3x = 4.97 doublings.
2. **Data.** Series 2016-2025: 337.4, 394.5, 388.9, 399.2, 572.0 (2020 legacy), 797.4, 1046.4,
   1462.8, 2625.9, 3194.0 t. Event `2025-01-01-jsr-payload-mass-2025` (legacy) 3,141 t for
   2025; `2025-01-01-spacex-share-of-mass-to-orbit` SpaceX ~85% (2025), 86% (2024), 60% (2020).
   Longer history (collector rows from 1957): ramp 1957-1968 (8 → 410 t) then a plateau at
   ~300-1,400 t from 1968 to 2019 (~50 years).
   Starship: flights 7, 8, 9 lost a stage (Jan-May 2025), flights 10 and 11 succeeded
   (Aug, Oct 2025). No operational payload flight in the ledger; no 2026 event at all.
3. **Model.** Launch mass grows exponentially (fleet × cadence × payload). Log-linear fits:
   - 2021-25: slope 0.3695 ± 0.0321 SE → +44.7%/yr, doubling 1.88 yr (fit 2025 = 3,334 t; the
     2025 point is 4% below the line).
   - 2016-20: slope 0.1068 ± 0.0364 → +11.3%/yr (previous window).
   - 2016-25: +30.0%/yr; 2011-25 +26.6%/yr (long-run, p90 side per §4.1 step 3).
   Central rate = the current window (+44.7%/yr). Named constraint (Falcon 9 cadence
   saturation, 2024→25 step only +21.6%) is real, but the hand-off to Starship has no measured
   ramp in the ledger, so it only widens the range (no stacking).
   **Measurement gap:** the 2026 total is not yet reported (JSR annual totals arrive after
   year-end); no faster-reporting series of the same outcome (YTD 2026 payload mass) is in
   the ledger → gather request. The gap is not treated as a slowdown; extrapolation runs
   from the last measured point (2025).
   Time to target (calendar-year totals, from 3,194 t):
   - +60%/yr → 7.33 yr → first 100-kt year 2033 (137 kt)
   - +52.5%/yr (upper 80% slope bound) → 8.16 yr → 2034
   - +44.7%/yr → 9.32 yr; 2034 total 88.8 kt (short), 2035 total 128.5 kt → **2035**
   - +41.5%/yr (≈ the 0.59 quantile, see 5) → 9.92 yr → 2035 (102.8 kt)
   - +30.0%/yr → 13.1 yr → 2039; +26.6% → 14.6 yr → 2040; +25% → 2041
   - +21.6%/yr (single 2025 step, not a trend) → 17.6 yr → 2043
4. **Cross-checks.**
   - Reference class: the 1957-68 exponential ramp (~+43%/yr) stalled at ~400 t for ~50 yr,
     so a long plateau below target is a real possibility → p_achieved < 1.
   - Proponent: SpaceX/NextBigFuture ~$100/kg Starship projection (`2025-08-01-starship-100-per-kg-projection`)
     implies thousand-flight/yr cadence; not dated, not used for timing.
   - Prev (2026-10-12, v3): p10 2033, p50 2035, p90 2041, no p_achieved.
5. **Chance and quantiles.** p_achieved 0.88: failure = growth plateaus well below 100 kt/yr
   through 2100 (the 1968-2019 precedent; demand beyond broadband constellations unproven;
   ~85% of mass from one provider; Kessler/regulatory limits). Even +5%/yr from 2025 reaches
   100 kt by 2096, so only a near-flat century fails → 0.12.
   MC (N=400k): log-slope split-normal, median 0.370, lower sd 0.105 (so −1.28 sd ≈ +25%/yr),
   upper sd 0.078 (+1.28 sd ≈ +60%/yr), floor 0.05; 12% never; first CY = 2025 + ceil(ln 31.3/slope).
   P(by 2100) 0.880; unconditional p10 2033, p50 2035 (conditional quantile 0.5/0.88 = 0.57),
   p90 null (p_achieved < 0.9). Conditional (if achieved) p10/p50/p90 2033/2035/2040.
   Status: p50 2035 <= 2036-10-13 → yellow; not green (p50 > 2031-10-13; no operational
   Starship precursor).
6. **Cruxes.** Earlier: JSR 2026 total >= ~4,600 t (on trend: 3194 × 1.447 = 4,622 t); a
   partial-2026 tally above trend; operational Starship payload flights (Starlink V3) at
   >= monthly cadence; ship reuse. Later: 2026 total <= ~3,800 t (second sub-trend year
   after +21.6%); more Starship losses; no second reusable heavy launcher ramping.

## The Export — product manufactured in space sold commercially on Earth (beyond novelty)

1. **Gap.** Qualitative: 0 → 1 commercial sale of a space-made product beyond novelty.
   Measurable precursor: in-space production with return to Earth. Ledger: Varda W-1 return
   (Feb 2024), five missions with five reentries (`2025-11-30-varda-five-missions`, W-5 launched
   28 Nov 2025), clinical trials named as next goal; Flawless Photonics > 12 km ZBLAN fiber on
   ISS, quality verification ongoing; Space Forge semiconductor payload returned (May 2025);
   Redwire heart tissue / 14 PIL-BOX crystallization runs. All legacy, proponent-sourced.
   No achievement meets the wording.
2. **Driver.** No series; the milestone is a first event, so modelled as competing paths with a
   structural floor (pharma regulatory programme) plus a hazard for materials. Launch supply
   (payload-mass-to-orbit +44.7%/yr, rockets series) lowers access cost but is a neighbouring
   quantity: no quantitative mapping to sale probability, so it does not enter the hazard.
3. **Model.**
   - Path A (Varda pharma, 505(b)(2)-type reformulation): trial start from 2026-10 +
     lognormal(median 0.6 yr, 80% 0.15-2.0). The ~11 months since the Nov 2025 statement
     without a trial-start event are an unmeasured gap (no 2026 events gathered), not a
     slip. Programme incl. FDA review lognormal(median 3.5 yr, 80% 2.3-5.5). P(path delivers a
     sale) 0.55 (approval rates of reformulations are high, but GMP manufacturing in orbit and
     commercial-quantity returns are unproven). Conditional A: p10/p50/p90 2029/2031/2033.
   - Path B (ZBLAN fiber, semiconductor crystals, other materials): flat 5%/yr first-sale
     hazard from 2027. Anchor: 0 qualifying sales in ~40 yr of microgravity materials work
     (1985-2025; not in ledger, general history: EOS electrophoresis, Shuttle crystal growth)
     → historical rate < 7.5%/yr (rule of three) at 10-50x higher launch costs; ZBLAN is now
     at km scale. 5%/yr is the central value; 3%/yr and 8%/yr are sensitivity cases.
   - Common mode: P(space manufacturing ever beats terrestrial for some product by 2100)
     0.93 (same 40-yr reference class; zero sales so far).
   MC earliest-of(A,B) (N=400k): p_achieved 0.920; unconditional p10 2028, p50 **2032**
   (P by end-2031 0.49, by end-2036 0.68), p90 2078 (long tail: after Varda, only the 5%/yr
   hazard remains). Conditional (if achieved) 2028/2031/2054.
   Sensitivity: P(A) 0.4 → p50 2033; 0.7 → 2031; hazard 3% → p50 2032, p_achieved 0.887
   (p90 null); 8% → 2031, p90 2058; common mode 0.85 → p50 2032; trial start median 1.2 yr → 2032.
4. **Cross-checks.** Reference class: drug reformulation 5-8 yr from proof of concept to
   market; first Varda return 2024 → 2029-32. Proponent "first drug sale late 2020s" (~2028.5,
   said ~end 2025) × 1.5-3 on the remaining 2.6 yr → 2029.8-2033.7. Market forecast
   ($4.6B by 2030, projection) cannot move status. Prev (v3) p50 2031, p10 2029, p90 2041
   with hazards rising 6→10%/yr and no common-mode failure.
5. **Result.** p_achieved 0.92, p10 2028, p50 2032, p90 2078. Status yellow: measurable
   progress (repeated manufacture-and-return), p50 2032 <= 2036-10-13; not green (p50 >
   made_at+5 and no precursor at commercial scale, no non-proponent corroboration).
6. **Cruxes.** Earlier: IND / trial start with a space-made formulation; positive ZBLAN quality
   result plus a paid supply contract; any paid offtake of a space-made material; checking
   whether NBS SRM 1960 (Shuttle-made latex spheres sold from 1985) meets the wording.
   Later: Varda pivots to reentry-as-a-service only; ZBLAN verification fails; no trial start
   reported by end-2027.

## The Homestead — permanent crewed base operates 1+ year using local resources (Moon or Mars)

1. **Gap.** Chain: crewed landing (none since 1972) → continuously crewed habitat → ISRU in
   service (oxygen/water/regolith) → 1 year of operation. ISRU: MOXIE 12 g/h O2 at Mars
   (`2023-09-06-moxie-completes-mission-rv`); 4 crew × 0.84 kg O2/day ≈ 140 g/h → ~12x MOXIE
   (~3.6 doublings); MRE O2 from simulant (May 2025); PRIME-1 drilled, no ice sampled; VIPER
   revived, launch late 2027. Life support: Tiangong 100% O2 regeneration, ISS 98% water,
   ISS food 100% resupplied, Lunar Palace 1 80% food for 105 days.
2. **Drivers.** Landing dates (proponent, discounted); lift capacity (payload-mass-to-orbit
   3,194 t in 2025, +44.7%/yr; a base needs ~10^2-10^3 t/yr, so lift is not binding).
   ISRU scale-up is ~12x, an engineering step, so it is folded into the landing-to-base gap.
3. **Model (structural floor chain, Monte Carlo, earliest programme wins).**
   - US: SpaceX internal ~Sep 2028 (said Oct 2025, 2.9 yr out; `2025-10-20-artemis-iii-landing-slips-2028`)
     × discount lognormal(median 1.75, 80% 1.3-2.6; Artemis III already slipped mid-2027 → 2028+)
     → landing p50 2030.9.
   - China: ~2030 (said Apr 2025, 4.7 yr; `2025-04-23-china-crewed-landing-2030`) × lognormal(median
     1.15, 80% 1.0-1.5; China's crewed programme has largely kept dates) → p50 2030.7.
   - Landing → permanent base with ISRU: lognormal(median 7.5 yr, 80% 4.5-13). Anchors: ILRS
     crewed landing 2030 → basic base 2035 = 5 yr × 1.5 discount; ISS first module 1998 →
     continuous crew 2000 (2 yr); Mir 1986 → 1989 (3 yr); Tiangong-1 2011 → continuously crewed
     Tiangong 2022 (11 yr). Plus a 1-year operating floor.
   - Abandonment of the base phase: US 0.30, China 0.25 (Apollo ended with no base;
     Constellation cancelled 2010; FY2026 SLS-termination proposal; no base is funded in
     either programme, only landings). If both abandon: 0.5 chance of a later programme
     (Moon or Mars) starting uniformly 2045-2070 + lognormal(median 10 yr).
   - Common mode: 0.92 that a 1-yr crewed base on local resources is feasible/affordable at all
     this century (radiation, food closure at 80% for 105 days only, ISRU never above g/h).
   N=400k: p_achieved 0.886; unconditional p10 2035, p50 **2039**, p90 null.
   Conditional (if achieved) 2035/2038/2044. P(by end-2036) 0.25.
   Sensitivity: gap median 5 yr (ILRS face value) → p50 2036; 10 yr → 2041; common mode
   0.85 → p_achieved 0.82, p50 2039; 0.97 → 0.935, p50 2038, p90 2055; abandonment
   0.4/0.35 → 2039.
4. **Cross-checks.** ILRS basic base 2035 (projection; its first phase is largely robotic)
   × 1.5 → ~2040. Artemis III mid-2027 → ≥2028 slip already. Prev (v3): p10 2035, p50 2038,
   p90 2044 with P(neither by 2060) 0.05 and only independent abandonment risk.
5. **Result.** p_achieved 0.89, p10 2035, p50 2039, p90 null. Red: p50 2039 > 2036-10-13 (Distant).
6. **Cruxes.** Earlier: orbital propellant-transfer demo in 2026-27; a crewed landing by 2030;
   a funded surface-habitat contract; ISRU O2 at >= 100 g/h; VIPER confirming accessible ice.
   Later: Artemis III re-dated past 2030; SLS/Artemis cancelled without replacement; ILRS
   crewed phase re-dated past 2040.
