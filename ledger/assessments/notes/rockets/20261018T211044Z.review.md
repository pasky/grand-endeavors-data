## MAJOR findings

### D1 — milestone:the-deluge — Environmental clearance is not a launch licence

The notes’ cross-check and the record’s `calc` and `blockers` describe **69 Starship launches/year as currently licensed**, adding 25 at Starbase to 44 at LC-39A. But `2026-01-29-faa-lc39a-starship-44-launches` explicitly says the final EIS/Record of Decision covers up to 44 launches and **SpaceX still needs an FAA licence**. Its verification note likewise distinguishes environmental approval from an operator licence. `2026-10-14-faa-starbase-456-launches-peis` supports the currently authorized 25 at Starbase, with 100/456 only proposed expansion stages.

Thus the ledger supports 25 currently authorized Starbase launches, plus environmental clearance for 44 at LC-39A—not 69 currently licensed launches. At the assumed 100 t/flight, the established authorization corresponds to 2.5 kt/year rather than the claimed roughly 7 kt/year. This is a factual error in the regulatory-capacity cross-check and blockers, even though licence counts are not direct simulation inputs.

**Fix:** distinguish current authorization, environmental clearance awaiting licensing, and proposed expansion throughout. Reassess the cross-check with LC-39A licensing still outstanding. The reproduced 2036 median need not change merely to repair this statement; any numerical change needs its own rule-7 justification.

### E1 — milestone:the-export — A correction’s explicitly removed mission count is restored

The notes’ Export data points say Varda had **five missions by November 2025**, citing `2025-11-30-varda-five-missions-rv`. That effective replacement supports only a handful of missions. Its verification note specifically says the source does **not** establish five completed missions or five successful reentries and that the five-mission count was removed. Using the replacement ID while reinstating the removed claim does not satisfy the effective-evidence rule.

The separate, later `2026-09-30-varda-series-d-251m` does support **six successful reentries** and **$251 million** raised, so the current demonstration-scale path does have support without the discarded historical count.

**Fix:** remove the November five-mission claim; retain the replacement’s qualitative wording and the independently supported September 2026 count. Do not derive a historical cadence from the corrected-away five. No change to the simulation is required unless that unsupported count actually informed an input.

### E2 — milestone:the-export — Unknown sales are promoted to a measured absence

The input register labels the materials hazard’s **2026.80** origin as a **measured absence of a first sale**. The rationale and gap discussion further assert that nothing space-manufactured has ever been sold on Earth. The cited `2026-05-13-varda-united-therapeutics` establishes only that Varda could not name such a product in May—not a comprehensive observation of every materials supplier through October 18. The framework itself explicitly discusses historical sales of NIST space beads, excluding them because ongoing repeated production is required, not because no sale happened.

The required quantity is absence of a **qualifying recurring product line**; absence of every first sale is a different and stronger claim. This matters computationally: `export()` starts a fresh first-sale hazard at 2026.80 and then adds a recurrence lag. The supplied sensitivity with the same pharma conditioning but the earlier hazard origin (`only trial-start conditioning`) gives **p50 2035, p_achieved 0.878**, versus **2036, 0.877** with the October origin. That comparison is not a recommended replacement forecast; it demonstrates the timing consequence of the unsupported measurement claim.

**Fix:** distinguish no qualifying sale reported in the ledger from measured worldwide non-occurrence. Label the current-state conditioning as assumed, explain why no potentially qualifying materials line is already between first sale and recurrence, and report an alternative-state/start-clock sensitivity. Narrow the public rationale to the actual milestone. If the evidence does not warrant conditioning on no first sale, update the distribution rather than silently retaining that condition as measured. A disclosed, reasoned assumption with sensitivity is acceptable; a global absence inferred from one company’s inability to name an example is not a measurement.
