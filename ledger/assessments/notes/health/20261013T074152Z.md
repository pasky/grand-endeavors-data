unbroken-mind are now judged at the frontier (the whole resident population of one 50M+ country or region).

within 80+. The milestones are judged against this wording.

(2026-04-07). That is 7.15 yr x ln2 / ln(20896) = a 0.50-yr doubling. It speeds discovery and design, but it

## Taming Cancer: 80% of all solid-tumour cases reach 10-year survival (frontier 50M+ population)

**1. Gap.** Ledger data (all legacy): US all-cancer 5-yr relative survival 69% (ACS 2025, diagnoses 2014-20);
breast 10-yr 86%; stage IV 5-yr 31% breast, 8.2% lung, 15.6% CRC; pancreatic 8%; Black-White gap 4-7 pts.
US 10-yr survival is about 0.69 x conditional 5->10 (0.85-0.95) = 0.59-0.66, central 0.62. Non-survival is therefore
0.38 against a 0.20 target: a 1.9x gap, ln = 0.64 (0.93 halvings).
Frontier alternative **(background)**: Korea (51.7M), KCCR 5-yr survival 72.9% for diagnoses 2018-22, so 10-yr is about
0.64-0.69 and non-survival 0.34, a 1.7x gap. That figure is thyroid-inflated but counts under the literal wording.

**2. Trend.** The milestone's own series is cohort survival. It lags: the latest 5-yr figure is for 2014-20
diagnoses, and there is no 10-yr all-site series in the ledger.
- Cohort 5-yr non-survival **(background ACS)**: 51% (1975-77) -> 33% (2008-14) -> 31% (2014-20). Long-run
  ln(51/31)/41 = 1.21%/yr; recent ln(33/31)/6 = 1.04%/yr (rounding range 0.5-1.6).
- **Faster-reporting series, explicit mapping** (v4 step 3). In a steady state, cancer deaths divided by cancer
  incidence (the age-adjusted M/I ratio) is the share of cases that eventually die of cancer: a period estimate of
  the same non-survival outcome. Dividing by incidence removes the "mortality falls when incidence falls" confound.
  **(background, SEER/ACS)**: the age-adjusted death rate fell ~1.5-1.6%/yr over 2012-2022 (166.5 -> 142/100k)
  while age-adjusted incidence was ~flat (-0.1%/yr), so M/I fell ~1.5%/yr. ACS projected counts give the same:
  0.415 (2005) -> 0.355 (2015) -> 0.303 (2025) = 1.56-1.58%/yr. The long-run 2005-2022 age-adjusted M/I is ~1.2%/yr.
  Korea's recent 5-yr non-survival: 29.7% (2014-18) -> 27.1% (2018-22) = 2.3%/yr; 1.1%/yr since 2011-15 (background).
- The gap since the last points (2020 cohort, 2022 period) is unmeasured, not flat. No newer data shows a stall,
  so I extrapolate from the last points.

**3. Model.** Non-survival declines exponentially (a constant proportional hazard improvement per year).
- **Median rate 1.5%/yr**: the US M/I period rate, the most current series of the outcome. The cohort rate of
  1.04%/yr is the slow case (rate p10 1.0%). Korea's median is 1.7%/yr (p10 1.1%).
- Composition: the residual non-survival concentrates in slow-improving sites. A two-group calculation (hard sites
  0.17 at 0.6%/yr plus the rest 0.21 at 2.2%/yr, the same 1.48%/yr start) crosses 0.20 in 49 yr, against 43 yr at a
  constant rate, so time is multiplied by U(1.00, 1.15). This is evidenced by site-specific survival (pancreas 8%,
  stage IV lung 8.2%).
- Structural floor: the period crossing P implies a cohort diagnosed ~P-2 (deaths concentrate early). That cohort
  completes 10-yr follow-up at P+8 and is published 1.5-3 yr later, so the lag is U(7, 12).
- Central US: 2019 + 0.629/0.015 = 2061 period, so ~2071 observed. Central Korea: 2022 + 0.531/0.017 = 2053, so ~2062 observed.
  The Korea level is background and thyroid-driven, so Korea counts as frontier with P 0.6.
- MC: US NS0 = 1-0.69xU(.85,.95) at 2019; Korea NS0 = 1-0.729xU(.88,.95) at 2022. Rates are lognormal and correlated
  (rho 0.7).
  **Result: p_achieved 0.965, p10 2052, p50 2066, p90 2088** (US-only: p50 2074, p90 2098, P 0.92).
  P(by 2031) is 0 and P(by 2036) is 0.

**4. Cross-checks.**
- Reference: US 5-yr survival rose +20 pts in 41 yr. Childhood cancer non-survival fell ~2.6%/yr (background, upper case):
  ln 1.9/0.026 = 25 yr, so period 2044 and observed ~2053, about p10.
- Proponent: the Cancer Moonshot goal of -50% age-adjusted death rate by 2047 (background) implies ~2.7%/yr of
  mortality decline. Discounted 1.5-3x, that is 0.9-1.8%/yr, giving ETAs of 2062-2095, consistent.
- The previous ETA (v3) was 2088, the US cohort rate 1.04%/yr only. My US-only slow case of 1.04%/yr gives 2090, the
  same, so the shift comes from the rate source and the frontier, not from padding.
- AI driver (METR 0.50-yr doubling; `2025-01-01-ai-drugs-75-in-trials`, legacy) stays out of the median.

**5.** p_achieved 0.97 (>= 0.9, so all quantiles are years). Status red (Distant), with p50 2066 > 2036.

**6. Cruxes.**
- Earlier: SEER/ACS releases showing all-site 5-yr survival rising >= 1 pt per release; period-analysis 10-yr survival
  >= 70% in any 50M+ population; durable stage IV survival with ADC, bispecific or mRNA-vaccine regimens; positive
  NHS-Galleri stage shift.
- Later: M/I decline slowing below 1%/yr; the NCI cut enacted; survival flat at 69-70%.
- Gather: an all-site 10-yr relative or period survival series (SEER, CRUK, KCCR, CONCORD) and US M/I by year.

## Precise Screening: >50% of new NCD diagnoses (case-weighted, 4 classes) caught pre-symptomatic

**1. Gap.** No ledger record measures any class share. Case weights use first diagnoses per year
**(background, US)**: cancer ~2.0M primaries (ACS 2025), first CVD diagnosis ~2.5M (1.8-3.5M: MI, stroke, HF,
AF, PAD; one per person), T2D ~1.4M (1.2-1.6M), neurodegeneration ~0.6M (0.5-0.9M). The weights are
~31 / 38 / 22 / 9%.
Current pre-symptomatic shares at the frontier **(background estimates)**:
- cancer 18-40%: US screen-detected breast, prostate, cervix and CRC plus incidental kidney and thyroid ~25%;
  Korea/Japan national screening higher;
- CVD 6-20%: silent AF, imaging atherosclerosis, AAA;
- T2D 50-80%: routine HbA1c;
- neuro 0-3%: the cleared pTau tests, `2025-05-01-fda-clears-lumipulse...-rv` and `2025-10-13-...elecsys...-rv`, are for
  symptomatic patients.
Case-weighted central: 0.31x0.25 + 0.38x0.12 + 0.22x0.65 + 0.09x0.01 = **0.27**, against >0.50: a 1.85x gap.
(The previous record's equal-class mean of ~23% is not the defined quantity.)

**2. Trend and drivers.** No series exists. The drivers are:
- MCED (PATHFINDER 2: >7x detection on top of USPSTF screening, 53.5% early stage, 99.5% specificity,
  sensitivity 73.7% only for the 12 deadliest cancers; Cancerguard launched 2025; NHS-Galleri results
  pending, a projection);
- AI imaging and ECG for CVD (873 FDA AI radiology devices, +115 in 2025, i.e. +15%/yr);
- routine HbA1c;
- preclinical AD (needs a preventive therapy; Kisunla 3-yr -27% progression is in symptomatic patients;
  posdinemab failed).

**3. Model.** Each class follows an S-curve after a floor:
- cancer: c0 + drift U(0.2-0.6) pts/yr (LDCT, CRC at 45, incidental imaging), plus MCED after approval and coverage
  U(2028, 2033), uptake logistic with cap U(0.25, 0.60), midpoint coverage + N(10, 4) (mammography 13 yr and CRC
  20 yr to ~70%), cases caught = uptake x 0.88 (share of cases at 50+) x episode sensitivity U(0.35, 0.60);
- CVD: logistic to cap U(0.35, 0.65), midpoint N(2042, 6), scale U(4, 7);
- T2D: the gap to 0.85 closes at U(1, 3)%/yr;
- neuro: a preventive AD therapy with P 0.35 in 2028-33, then midpoint approval + N(8, 3), else N(2052, 8); cap U(0.2, 0.5);
- measurement: published 1-3 yr after the crossing.
Median path: 2025 0.27, 2030 0.30, 2035 0.34, 2040 0.38, 2045 0.43, 2050 0.47, 2060 0.52.
The CVD class (38% of cases) is decisive. A small neuro class is outweighed, as the framework says it can be.
**MC: p_achieved 0.969, p10 2049, p50 2057, p90 2078.** P(by 2036) is 0.

**4. Cross-checks.**
- Reference: US mammography went from ~29% to ~70% in ~13 yr after 1987; CRC screening from ~35% to ~70% in ~20 yr; lung
  LDCT is at 16-18% after 9 yr (background). With MCED coverage ~2030, that gives 2043-50 for cancer uptake, before
  sensitivity limits.
- Proponent: GRAIL's PMA "H1 2026" (legacy projection), discounted 1.5-3x, gives approval in 2027-29, consistent with the floor.
- The previous ETA (v3) was 2054. Mine is 3 yr later, because the case weighting gives CVD its real weight, partly offset by a
  frontier cancer share above England-style estimates.

**5.** p_achieved 0.97. Status red (Distant), with p50 2057 > 2036.

**6. Cruxes.**
- Earlier: FDA approval plus Medicare coverage of an MCED; an NHS-Galleri stage-shift or mortality benefit; reimbursed
  opportunistic AI coronary-calcium or AI-ECG reporting at scale; a preventive anti-amyloid success (AHEAD 3-45,
  TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 3).
- Later: a null NHS-Galleri result; USPSTF/ESC against incidental-finding reporting; coverage refused.
- Gather: the measured screen-detected or incidental share per class (Routes to Diagnosis, SEER detection mode,
  undiagnosed-diabetes share, silent-AF share) and MCED test volumes.

## Unbroken Mind: dementia (CDR>=1) prevalence 80+, age-standardized (WHO std), < 5% in a 50M+ population

**1. Gap.**
- `2025-01-01-nhs-uk-dementia-prevalence-80-plus-rv` (corrected): "1 in 6 over 80" in the UK (67M), = 16.7%. The
  figure matches the CFAS II era (fieldwork 2008-11, background) for all-cause dementia. The ledger wording says
  "Alzheimer's", so the AD-only reading (all-cause 20-26%) gets weight 0.25.
- The WHO standard within 80+ is younger (80-84 59%, 85-89 28%, 90+ 13%) than the UK mix (50/32/18), so
  standardizing gives ~15%.
- US ADAMS (fieldwork 2001-03; legacy): 24% at 80-89 and 37% at 90+, standardized ~0.257.
- SHARE 2022, 65+: 4.5-22.7% by country (legacy, not 80+).
Frontier start ~0.16 (0.14-0.26) at ~2012, against 0.05: a 3.2x gap, ln = 1.16.
The years since ~2012 (and since 2002 for ADAMS) are unmeasured, not flat (v4). I extrapolate from those points.

**2. Trend.**
- Incidence (`2024-01-01-lancet-commission...`, legacy): -20% over 2-3 decades = 0.74-1.12%/yr.
- Prevalence, the milestone's own quantity **(background)**: UK CFAS I -> II, 65+ age-standardized 8.3% -> 6.5% over
  1991 -> 2011 = 1.2%/yr; US HRS 65+ 11.6% -> 8.8% (2000-12) = 2.3%/yr crude, ~1.5-2% age-adjusted; Framingham
  incidence ~20%/decade = 2.2%/yr.
- Median **1.3%/yr**: the frontier's own prevalence series (CFAS), between the Lancet incidence and HRS rates.
  Lognormal, p10 0.8%/yr.
- Regime: a preventive therapy (evidence: intensive BP control RR 0.85 in 33,995 adults; donanemab -27%
  progression; lecanemab modelled 3.5-8.3-yr delay; etalanetug -90% p-tau; against that, the 2025 failures of
  oral semaglutide and posdinemab). Modelled as a hazard of 2.5%/yr from 2030, giving P(by 2045) 0.31 and
  P(by 2060) 0.53. The regime adds a lognormal(median 1.0%, p90 2.5%/yr) extra decline from T+5 (the prevalence lag).
- Measurement: survey cadence plus publication, U(1, 5) yr.

**3. Calculation.**
- Without the regime, central: 2012 + ln(0.16/0.05)/0.013 = 2101.
- **MC: p_achieved 0.659, p10 2065, p50 2090, p90 null**; the conditional median (if achieved) is 2080. P(by 2036) is 0.

**4. Cross-checks.**
- Framingham-like 2.2%/yr sustained: ln 3.2/0.022 = 53 yr, so 2065 (= p10).
- US path: ADAMS 0.257 (2002) at the HRS ~2%/yr gives 2084; at 1.3%/yr, 2128.
- The previous ETA (v3) was 2096 with P 0.55. It started the trend in 2020 from 2001-03 and 2010s levels and used the
  slowest rate (0.93%/yr) as the median.

**5.** p_achieved 0.66 (0.5-0.9), so p90 is null. Status red (Distant), with p50 2090 > 2036.

**6. Cruxes.**
- Earlier: AHEAD 3-45 or TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 3 showing >= 30% incidence reduction; new prevalence waves (CFAS III, HRS-HCAP
  2022, Japan) with a decline >= 2%/yr.
- Later: prevention trials failing; obesity or diabetes reversing the decline; longer survival with dementia raising
  prevalence.
- Gather: an 80+ age-specific prevalence series for a 50M+ country.

## The Long Middle Age: validated >=10-yr biological-age reversal (clocks + function) + corresponding disease reduction

**1. Gap.**
- Best human reversals: 2.5 yr (TRIIM 2019, n=9), 3.23 (Fitzgerald 2021, 8-wk pilot), 2.6 (Buck TPE+IVIG RCT 2025).
  Against >= 10 yr, that is a 3.1-4x gap on the clocks.
- Functional biomarkers: no human trial reports a comparable functional reversal.
- "Corresponding" disease reduction: with an incidence doubling time of ~8 yr, 2^(10/8) = 2.4x lower incidence (~58%
  cut). The best outcome trials reach ~20-25% (SELECT -20% MACE, background).

**2. Trend.** The clock series is flat: the 2025 point (2.6) is below 2021 (3.23). That is a newer measurement below
trend, so the stall is evidenced, not a gap. The ETA is tied to the reprogramming driver:
- SB000 7.42 yr in vitro (preprint, legacy);
- GPT-4b micro factors at >50x marker expression with a ~50% hit rate (legacy; METR 0.5-yr doubling as the AI-design driver);
- ER-100 first in human Q1 2026 (announcement, local eye; no dosing event in the ledger);
- RTR242 dosed in Dec 2025 (autophagy, not reprogramming);
- NewLimit $130M and Retro ~$1B (announcements);
- semaglutide lowering 11 organ clocks (Phase IIb);
- mouse lifespan +34.9% (rapamycin+trametinib) and +36.6% (ITP);
- negative: UNITY liquidated after a senolytic Phase 2b miss.

**3. Model.** A structural-floor chain:
- Stage A (systemic human >= 10-yr clock plus functional reversal) = 2026 + lognormal(median 8, p90 18) yr, giving
  p10 2030, p50 2034, p90 2044. The steps are local safety, a systemic IND (~3-4 yr), a ~2-yr efficacy readout, and
  iteration. The p90 is wider than the previous 16 yr, because no primate systemic-delivery result is in the ledger.
- Stage B = lognormal(median 8, p90 11): start ~1-2 yr, then a >= 5-yr outcome trial (SELECT ~5, TAME ~6), then
  replication.
- P(no modality succeeds by 2100) = 0.30: the flat human record, UNITY, ~90% clinical attrition, an unprecedented
  effect size; partly offset by ~5 funded programmes and AI design.
- **MC: p_achieved 0.70, p10 2037, p50 2046, p90 null**; the conditional median is 2042. P(by 2036) is 0.06.

**4. Cross-checks.**
- Reference: CAR-T went from first in human to approval in 7 yr (2010-17); GLP-1 from approval to the SELECT outcome
  in 18 yr; gene therapy from first in human to approval in 27 yr. 2026 + 14-20 = 2040-46.
- Proponent "this decade" (background), discounted 1.5-3x, puts A at 2032-38; + 8 gives 2040-46.
- The previous ETA (v3) was 2046. The chain is unchanged in substance; only the stage-A tail is wider and
  p_achieved is now explicit.

**5.** p_achieved 0.70 (< 0.9), so p90 is null. Status red (Distant), with p50 2046 > 2036.

**6. Cruxes.**
- Earlier: ER-100 safety and efficacy readouts and a systemic reprogramming IND; any human trial with >= 5-yr reversal
  across several clocks plus function; an outcome trial with >= 40% multi-disease reduction.
- Later: teratoma or loss-of-identity signals; regulators refusing ageing as an indication; more senolytic-style
  failures.
- Gather: 2026 human clock-reversal results; ER-100 dosing status; the status of the TAME and PEARL outcome trials.
