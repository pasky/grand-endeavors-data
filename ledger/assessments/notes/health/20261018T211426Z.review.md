### 1. milestone:taming-cancer — the qualifying move is not truthfully attributed to new information

The change_note attributes the p10 change **2055 → 2056** to new evidence `2026-04-22-seer-april-2026-release-five-year-survival`, replacing 69% at cohort midpoint 2017 with 70.5% at midpoint 2019. The staged notes acknowledge the old sensitivity, but do not identify what new information warrants promoting it to the median, or identify the previous central-anchor choice as an error under the most-current-comparable-series rule.

There is also a consequential precision change within the replacement anchor: the all-cancer sampling band becomes **[.7045,.7055]**, whereas the prior 70.5% sensitivity used **[.700,.710]**. Thus the narrowing is material to the sole stored calendar-year move and deserves explicit attribution, not merely the explanation about fast draws having expected more progress by 2019.

**Fix:** distinguish newly ledgered evidence from information already used in the previous working. Either establish genuinely new information that changes the previous choice, or explicitly identify and justify the previous central-anchor/precision errors under rule 7(b), including why the older central anchor violated §4.1 step 3. Attribute the level, cohort-date and uncertainty-width changes and their effects. A corrected attribution may justify retaining the staged numbers; this finding does not require reverting to an older observation.

### 2. milestone:the-long-middle-age — the purported trend sensitivity does not implement its own arithmetic

The new cross-check extrapolates a clock-effect envelope from **2.5 years at 2019.9** to **3.5 years at 2026.4**. Its script correctly obtains log growth **.05176496/year** and **20.2806 further years** to reach a 10-year effect: capability arrival **2046.6806**. But the notes and calc then describe **Amed=14** as the duration implied by this trend, producing p50 **2050.33**, P **.7427**.

Stage A starts at **2025** in this model. A 14-year duration therefore centres capability arrival at **2039**, not 2046.68. Faithfully translating the stated envelope into this same sensitivity requires **Amed=21.6806**. With all other inputs and draws unchanged, that gives p10 **2041.73**, p50 **2060.55**, P **.7192**. The claimed trend-derived sensitivity is therefore approximately **ten years too early** in its reported median.

**Fix:** run and report the correctly translated envelope sensitivity, clearly retaining its caveat that these unlike studies are not a fitted capability series. Alternatively, describe 14 years as a separate assumed duration with its own justification, not as the calculated envelope result. The central Amed=8 forecast need not move: this is an arithmetic/mapping error in a substantial reported sensitivity, not an objection to the disclosed central judgment.
