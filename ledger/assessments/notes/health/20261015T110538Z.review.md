## MAJOR 1 — milestone:unbroken-mind: incorrect reference-class denominator

**Where:** eta-notes.md, R6 and the unbroken-mind F-stall derivation; the record's rationale, calc and change_note.

The notes justify a 15% stall probability with one non-declining series out of eleven: seven declining cohorts from Wolters 2020, plus CFAS prevalence, HRS, NHATS and Hisayama. They then use Laplace smoothing, 2/13 = .154, as the derivation of .15. The simulated stall contributes .1123 of unconditional failure, so the counted-reference-class requirement applies.

**The seventh measured trend does not exist.** [Wolters 2020, PMC7455342](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7455342/) includes seven cohorts for incidence levels, but its time-trend analysis has only six: FHS, Rotterdam, Three-City, Gothenburg, PAQUID and CFAS. AGES–Reykjavik supplies incidence levels, not a secular trend. This is explicit in the Methods' eligibility restriction to cohorts with at least two epochs and visible in Figure 3A. The paper's statement that declines occur across the analyzed cohorts does not establish a seventh trend. Saved source and figure: `review-work/wolters.md` and `review-work/wolters-fig3.jpg`.

Even accepting the notes' other counting choices, the class therefore contains **one non-declining series out of ten**, not eleven: Laplace 2/12 = .1667. CFAS incidence and prevalence also come from the same population; treating these as separate exchangeable observations requires justification. Deduplicating CFAS would leave nine population units and give 2/11 = .1818, though neither calculation resolves the additional assumed transfer from mixed incidence/crude-prevalence trends to a future sustained 80+ standardized-prevalence stall.

**Numerical relevance:** on the unchanged common draws, substituting .1667 for .15 gives P = .6750 and p50 = 2080.25, versus .6829 and 2079.61. Calendar-year p50 changes from 2079 to 2080. Using .1818 gives .6674 and 2080.84. These are diagnostic sensitivities, not a recommendation to mechanically adopt either probability (`review-work/extra-output.txt`).

**Fix:** enumerate the actual observed trends, exclude AGES from the trend denominator, address CFAS overlap, and label the transfer to this failure scenario as assumed. Either retain .15 as an explicitly reasoned judgment supported only approximately by the corrected class, or recalibrate and attribute the resulting moves. Correct the record's claim that .15 is now derived from 1/11. A missing trend must remain unmeasured, not be counted as a decline (RUBRIC §4.1 steps 5 and 7).
