The advance for `2026-01-16-nous-209-lynch-syndrome-vaccine-phase-1b-2` says cancer prevention has not been tested. The staged assessment reintroduces that corrected chronology.

**Fix:** say that ER-100's first dosing was announced on 9 June, and that KHL reports dosing on 11 March. Remove the assertion that KHL was earlier than ER-100 unless an actual ER-100 dosing date is sourced. Avoid claiming either intervention was globally first. The rationale's description of the regulated trial's first three patients and the yellow status can remain.
