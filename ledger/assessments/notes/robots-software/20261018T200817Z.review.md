**Fix:** use the matching v1.1 task lengths/results to justify the benchmark-gap statistic, or explicitly retain a representative-effort value as an assumption rather than a measured gap and test plausible alternatives. Recompute the kernel mixture if its endpoint changes, and attribute that change as an evidence/error correction. Do not substitute a new endpoint geometric mean and call that a measured horizon.
