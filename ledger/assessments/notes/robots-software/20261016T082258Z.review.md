## MAJOR findings

### 1. milestone:the-nature-author — the shadow-evaluation bound is attached to models that were not tested

The model sets `SHADOW = "2026-07-29"`, reads the date-frontier blended horizon **1.026 h**, and imposes `m > log2(1.026 / 0.598) = 0.779`. The notes and calc describe this as a capability-derived lower bound, rather than an assumed transfer. This truncation is a new input attributed to audit r2 Nature #3.

Event `2026-07-29-shadow-evals-agents-fail-open-ended-research` establishes two unsuccessful research cases; it does not establish a horizon threshold. Its linked primary paper, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27191v1, identifies **Opus 4.8/OpenClaw** as the model in both main experiments, with a **GPT-5.6 Sol/Codex** robustness experiment. Its limitations explicitly say the strongest Anthropic model, Fable 5, could not be tested. Conversely, the ledger observation underlying **1.026 h on 2026-07-24** combines **Opus 5's software frontier (4.135 h)** and **Fable 5's remote frontier (0.255 h)**. Publication date is not the tested models' capability level. The Opus 4.8 remote observation is **0.095 h on 2026-05-28**, not 0.255 h.

Moreover, two failures on assigned NeurIPS research questions do not by themselves measure a universal lower bound on the first fully AI main-track acceptance. Their transfer to that event requires an assumption even after matching models correctly. Calling the evidence weak and showing a no-bound sensitivity does not make the asserted measured constraint true.

**Effect:** the staged result is **2028.45 / 2030.16 / 2041.58**, `p_achieved ≈ 0.908`. Changing only the truncation to zero while retaining its random stream gives **2028.11 / 2029.71 / 2039.98**. The existing no-bound sensitivity uses a different stream and gives **2028.11 / 2029.71 / 2040.02**. Thus this input changes the median calendar year and materially changes p90; the near-2040 boundary also warrants numerical-stability checking after correction.

**Fix:** match the evaluations to the models actually tested; do not assign the July release-frontier horizon to them. Treat the transfer from these two research failures to the milestone's autonomy deficit as explicitly assumed, with a justified mapping and sensitivity, rather than an observed hard bound. Recompute the forecast and rule-7 attribution. The zero-bound probe diagnoses the effect; it is not a prescribed replacement forecast. Supporting source capture and probe are in `review-work/shadow.md` and `review-work/probes.{py,out}`.

**Fix:** reconcile the governing instructions before committing: use the single-success reliability specified by the binding rubric, with an explicit correction attribution, or obtain a formal applicable exception to that rubric. Do not silently reselect a reliability assumption under rule 7. This finding is about the rules conflict, not a private refit of the shared driver. Nature has the same documentary conflict, but its relative-ladder calculation cancels a constant 50/80 conversion, so it has no separate numerical finding here.
