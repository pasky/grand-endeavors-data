The milestone's own quantity (largest accepted AI-submitted patch) has no series. Mapping
(RUBRIC §4.1 step 2's own example): the human effort a 10k-line substantive diff represents,
against the METR task horizon.
- Effort of a 10k-line kernel-grade diff: a new driver/subsystem of that size is typically
  ~0.5-1 person-year of an experienced developer at ~30-60 final, reviewed lines/day; a diff
  that is partly tables/headers/mechanical conversion is cheaper; "equivalent critical infra"
  broadens the eligible projects. Central 1,200 h (72,000 min); p10 400 h, p90 4,000 h.
- Reliability level: 50% horizon. One acceptance suffices; the submitter can retry, select,
  and iterate with reviewer feedback across revisions (§4.1 "one accepted patch").

- Gap: 72,000 / 1,044.78 = 68.9x = 6.11 doublings (1,500 h would be 86.1x = 6.43).
