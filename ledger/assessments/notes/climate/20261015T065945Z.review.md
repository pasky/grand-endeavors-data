## MAJOR findings

### 1. Both `milestone:the-balance` and `milestone:the-ceiling`: assumed inventory reconciliation labelled measured

Both input registers call the 2025 non-CO₂ level **16.0 GtCO₂e a measured split**. The operands are evidenced, but their interpretation as a non-CO₂ measurement is not:

- `2026-09-01-edgar-2026-ghg-2025` reports **54.1 GtCO₂e**, all GHG excluding LULUCF. It does not report the CO₂/non-CO₂ split.
- `2026-05-13-gcb-2025-final-fossil-co2-record` reports **38.1 GtCO₂**, fossil fuels and cement **net of cement carbonation**, from a different inventory.
- Their difference is arithmetically 16.0, but identifying that entire residual as non-CO₂ assumes compatible inventory coverage/accounting. The notes themselves acknowledge the inventory mismatch and absence of EDGAR's own CO₂ component. Absence of contradictory evidence does not make that compatibility measured. The latest Bend record (`assess:20261014T161054Z`) correctly calls this same 16.0 an assumed split.

This violates §4.1 steps 3 and 7: a measurement of the operands is not a measurement of the mapping. The model uses this residual in both the emissions trajectory and Ceiling's GWP* calculation. The added sensitivity is useful but does not repair the false input label. Its reproduced results are Balance **.2702 → .2704**, Ceiling **.1769 → .1751** for 16 → 15 GtCO₂e; p50 remains null. The finding is the explicit assumed-as-measured classification, **not** the choice of a disclosed central estimate or the small sensitivity effect.

**Fix:** retain the evidenced operands, but label the residual **assumed cross-inventory non-CO₂ estimate**, state the compatibility assumption, and describe the +0.614%/yr rate as model-inferred rather than an independently measured gas-specific trend. Keep the existing sensitivity; preferably complete it on both sides or obtain a harmonised gas-resolved inventory. No central-value change is required merely to correct provenance; do not replace the existing judgment without a rule-7 basis. For reference, an additional common-draw 17 GtCO₂e probe gives Balance **.2695 / p10 2088.2**, Ceiling **.1762 / p10 2082.6**, both p50 null.
