## milestone:the-balance

Literal target: **Global net anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions reach approximately zero.** This is not net-zero fossil CO2, zero atmospheric CO2 growth, or balancing emissions with the natural ocean/land sink.

### 1. Gap and units

EDGAR 2026 reports 54.1 GtCO2e/year excluding LULUCF in 2025, +0.7% within that report's inventory vintage (`2026-09-01-edgar-2026-ghg-2025`). The previous EDGAR report gives 53.2 for 2024 (`2024-01-01-edgar-global-ghg-2024-rv`); 54.1/53.2 − 1 = 1.69% is NOT the within-vintage growth rate and is not used. GCB gives net land-use CO2 of about 4.1 Gt/year in 2025, total CO2 42.2 versus 42.4 in 2024 (`2026-05-13-gcb-2025-total-co2-slightly-lower`). Thus 54.1 + 4.1 = roughly 58.2 GtCO2e/year is an accounting proxy for all-GHG net anthropogenic emissions, not a newly measured harmonised inventory. It may omit land-use non-CO2 and differ in GWP conventions; its uncertainty matters.

A ratio to exactly zero is undefined. In the forecast, residual emissions R after deep abatement are 4–8 GtCO2e/year (central 6); these are explicitly elicited scenarios, NOT measured residual floors or an official definition of approximately zero. Gross abatement from 58.2 to 6 requires a 9.7-fold reduction, 89.69%. Additional anthropogenic net durable removals must then match all 6 GtCO2e/year, including offsets of remaining methane and N2O under a consistent GWP convention. Achieving CO2 neutrality alone does not satisfy this test.

### 3. Model and time calculation

Use a log-curved emissions transition, not linear extrapolation of 58 Gt to zero: E(t) = E0 exp(g t − a t²/2), with a residual floor R, and additional removal capacity C(t) = min(R, C0 exp(r t)). The net flow is max(R, E(t)) − C(t); approximate balance occurs when abatement reaches R AND additional effective removals reach R. Parallel work therefore takes max(t_abatement, t_removal), not their sum. R is a gross residual floor in this model, not an assertion that current EDGAR/GCB already subtracts these same new removals. Existing land-use flows are in the initial net level; additional removal must not be double-counted.

Central R = 6 and C0 = 0.0012. Solve t_abatement = [g + sqrt(g² + 2a ln(E0/R))]/a = 79.246911 years, or 2105.746911. Removal ratio is 6/0.0012 = 5,000; t_removal = ln(5000)/r = 58.934022 years, or 2085.434022. Central completion is 2105.746911, already beyond 2100. It is not legitimate to extrapolate an exponential to exactly zero without a compensating removal flow. Neither renewable capacity nor low electricity prices alone ensures all-GHG balance.

Uncertainty: 100,000 independent draws, Python random seed 142122; g normal(mean ln1.003, SD .003), a lognormal(median .0008001519275, log SD .50), R uniform(4,8), C0 lognormal(median .0012, log SD 1), r normal(mean .1445208207, SD .05). A 15% elicited structural failure branch represents persistent non-CO2/accounting/permanence barriers; r ≤ 0 also fails. All parameters are independent for this simple model, an important limitation. The curvature uncertainty covers roughly a factor 1.9 either side at the central 80%, including the faster total-CO2 value. The distributions and failure mass are forecasting judgments, not fitted standard errors. Failure is probability mass beyond the century, not a delay stacked onto every successful case.

Output: P(by 2100) = 0.23419; unconditional p10 = 2089.955788; p50 = null; p90 = null. The median only among outcomes completed by 2100 is 2091.438001, which is NOT the unconditional median. ### 3. Growth model and literal confirmation

Use log E(t) = log E0 + gt − at²/2. This extrapolates fractional emissions changes in log space; the linear change is in the logarithmic growth rate, not in the emissions level. Physical peak at g − at = 0 gives t_peak = g/a = 3.743675 years after 2026.5, calendar 2030.243675.

A physically flat derivative is not definitive recognition. Operational forecasting test: at least three post-peak annual observations and a cumulative drop of about 1%, followed by one reporting cycle. The 1% is a declared detection heuristic, not a replacement for the framework's literal achievement requirement. The reference class is GCB's false fossil plateau: 2014 34.78, 2015 34.71, 2016 34.69 Gt, a two-year drop of only 0.259%, followed by 35.26 in 2017 and 36.00 in 2018. Pandemic 2019 36.32 → 2020 34.38 then 2021 36.04 and 2022 36.72 demonstrates that a shock-driven decline, even a large one, alone is not proof. Recent China's 21-month plateau remaining open reinforces the multi-year requirement. One percent is roughly four times the false-plateau drop; it is not a statistical confidence bound and remains a forecast crux.

After the peak, E/E_peak = exp(−a k²/2). For a 1% fall, k = sqrt[−2 ln(.99)/a] = 5.012092 years; the three-year persistence floor is nonbinding centrally. Add one annual inventory reporting cycle, justified by the 2025 results appearing in April–September 2026. ETA = 2030.243675 + max(3, 5.012092) + 1 = 2036.255768. There is no separate arbitrary non-CO2 alignment lag, construction lag or confidence padding. The inventory lag and cumulative-decline check serve different purposes; actual achievement would still require a verified primary-source event satisfying all-GHG and definitive wording.

Uncertainty: 100,000 draws, seed 142121; g normal(ln1.003, SD .003), a lognormal(median .0008001519275, log SD .45), and an elicited 5% persistent failure branch. For negative sampled growth the inferred peak can precede the mid-2026 anchor; recognition is bounded below by 2029 (even a possible 2025 peak needs three completed post-peak annual observations through 2028 and reporting in 2029). This does NOT assert an already verified peak. Every other date uses the peak plus max(3, calculated decline interval) plus one year. The fixed 1% detection heuristic and independent parameter errors are modelling limitations.

Output: P(by 2100) .95016; unconditional p10 2031.226428, p50 2036.101156, p90 2046.757422. Conditional median among outcomes completed by 2100 is 2035.792612 and is NOT used as p50. Record .95, 2031, 2036, 2047. Median 2036 is within ten years of 2026-10-13, supported by measured clean-power displacement: yellow/Progressing, not achieved. It is not green (median beyond five years and no funded, demonstrated, independently corroborated programme covering the entire global GHG transition).

### 5–6. Decision and cruxes

Yellow, ETA ~2036; not achieved. Ember's verified achievement concerns electricity only. No verified achievement establishes a definitive peak of all greenhouse gases. Priority observations: full-year 2026 and 2027 matched-vintage all-GHG totals, TRACE revisions and scope, methane/N2O and net land-use trends, persistent global declines through Hormuz and ENSO recovery, and clean generation continuing to exceed demand growth. Earlier if those declines persist; later if a rebound sets a fresh record or fossil curvature fails to transfer to non-CO2 gases. A harmonised uncertainty estimate and a more defensible definitive-peak recognition criterion could move the range substantially.
