## MAJOR finding

### 1. milestone:the-bend — an unsupported all-GHG record is treated as measured history

The notes (§3, input register, and Appendix B; lines 98, 112 and 525) and the record's `calc` justify the 2029 recognition floor with a **measured 2025 record**. The simulation also searches for the emissions peak only from 2025.5 onward. This is not established for the milestone's stated quantity, global all-GHG emissions including net land use.

The effective evidence distinguishes the scopes:

- `2026-09-01-edgar-2026-ghg-2025` reports **54.1 GtCO₂e, +0.7%**, expressly **excluding LULUCF**. Its same-vintage implied 2024 value is 54.1 / 1.007 = **53.72393**, not the **53.2** from the older EDGAR edition in `2024-01-01-edgar-global-ghg-2024-rv`.
- Effective `co2-emissions-total-gcb` minus `co2-emissions-fossil-gcb` gives net land use **4.58 Gt in 2024** and **4.10 in 2025**.
- Applying the assessment's own inventory-combination method therefore gives **58.30393 → 58.20 GtCO₂e**, a **0.178% fall**, not a measured all-GHG record in 2025. The explicit historical-record claim in `2026-05-13-gcb-2025-final-fossil-co2-record` is for fossil CO₂ only.

This cross-inventory calculation does not establish a definitive global peak either; it establishes that the claimed measured premise is unsound. The fitted/smoothed 2025 land-use level of 4.443 is not an observation that repairs it. An assumed earliest peak is being presented as measured, with consequences for the recognition test and lower tail.

**Diagnostic:** 1,052/10,000 central draws assign the physical peak to the grid's first point, 2025.5. A bounded sensitivity adding a 2024.5 historical peak candidate using the above 2024/2025 ratio, and removing the unsupported 2025 floor, changes decimal p10/p50 from **2031.4/2036.8 to 2031.0/2036.7**; P remains .9505. This diagnostic assumes that the historical ratio transfers to each simulated baseline; it is **not** a proposed replacement forecast. Stored calendar quantiles happen to remain unchanged. See `review-work/history-check.py` and its output.

**Fix:** reconstruct the historical all-GHG peak with consistent inventory vintages and scope, include admissible earlier peaks in the recognition calculation, and derive the earliest recognition date from that history. Where the history cannot be reconciled, disclose the boundary as an assumption and test it rather than calling it measured. Qualify the EDGAR growth statement as excluding LULUCF. Attribute any resulting ETA changes to this input/scope correction under rule 7. This does not imply achievement now: the reconstructed decline is below the required 1%, and the recognition period has not elapsed.
