Renewables overtaking coal and clean generation covering all demand growth support the disclosed power-sector regime-change interpretation; they do not establish an economy-wide emissions peak.

The previous factual overreach is fixed: `2026-09-01-edgar-2026-ghg-2025` measures GHG **excluding land use**, 54.1 GtCO2e / +0.7%; the label instead uses GCB's fossil-CO2 record, 38.1 Gt / +1.0%. ### challenge:permanent-removal — yellow, Inching
