## MAJOR findings

### 1. `milestone:the-balance`, `milestone:the-ceiling` — CCS evidence does not measure the newly claimed reference class

The new justification for S1 (10% removal stall) counts zero stalls among six policy-revenue-only abatement services. Its sixth member is climate-motivated fossil CCS, starting with Sleipner at approximately 1 Mt/yr in 1996. Both `calc` strings and the notes then use growth to 41 Mt/yr over 29 years, `ln(41)/29 = 0.128/yr`, to classify that member as a scaling success and support retaining S1 = 0.10.

But `2025-01-01-cdr-fyi-capture-capacity-gap` explicitly reports **41 Mt/yr of total CCS capacity, all types**. It does not report 41 Mt/yr for the policy-only, climate-motivated subset. Combining a subset's starting point with the whole sector's endpoint does not establish that subset's growth rate. Calling the event legacy, or the class selection assumed, does not fix the scope mismatch. This is a factual evidence-to-quantity error in the new v6 failure-scenario justification, not an objection to retaining a disclosed 10% judgment.

**Fix:** supply comparable endpoints for the stated subset, or remove this growth calculation and substantiate the class membership/outcomes with appropriate sources. Alternatively, define a broader class honestly and disclose the assumed transfer from its revenue structure to durable removal. Give auditable inclusion/outcome criteria for the count. If no suitable countable class exists, explain why and retain a disclosed judgment with sensitivity, as §4.1 step 5 permits. Do not change S1 merely to obtain a different judgment: retain 0.10 if the repaired justification supports it, otherwise attribute and rerun the change under rule 7. Existing S1 sensitivities reproduce: 0.05/0.25 gives Balance P = 0.2957/0.2330 and Ceiling P = 0.1840/0.1782, with null medians throughout.
