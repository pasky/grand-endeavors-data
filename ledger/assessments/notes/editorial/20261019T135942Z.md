## robots-software

Label "On track: 1.79 h (Sep 2026), doubling every ~2.9 months and not slowing": level,
pace, and the comparison the status rests on, in plain words. Fine; kept.

Rationale, sentence by sentence:
1. "The horizon rose from about 3 minutes in June 2025 to 1.79 hours in September 2026,
   doubling roughly every 2.9 months." Level and pace, with the start point that makes the
   pace vivid. Good, but it never says what "1.79 hours" is: a reader who knows only the
   definition needs "tasks that take a skilled person about 1.8 hours, done reliably". Also
   "3 minutes" is a figure the record does not contain (its start point is 0.043 h), so the
   proposal says "a few minutes".
2. "The status asks whether it keeps its own pace: the later half of that period doubled no
   more slowly than the earlier half (about 2.6 vs 3.0 months, a difference within the
   noise), so the status is green." This is the rule and its windows, told to the reader:
   halves, a second pace figure, "within the noise". §6 says not to describe the rule or the
   halves; the label already gives the pace once. What the reader needs is why there is no
   outside pace (none exists) and what the comparison found (no slower than before).
3. "The main caveat: the remote-work component's conversion to hours rests on an assumed
   curve; that shifts the pace of both periods nearly alike, so it hardly affects the
   comparison." A caveat that dismisses itself, the exact §6 pattern. The uncertainty
   matters for the level (the hours are converted from indices, not measured), and that is
   what the reader should take away; the "cancels out of the status" part belongs in the
   basis, where it already is.

Verdict: change the rationale; keep the label. The new text says what the level means,
states the own-pace test as a finding, and keeps the caveat as a statement about the level.

   may have missed earlier deployments, so the speed-up is suggestive rather than proven."
   A caveat that changes the takeaway (the green rests on 8 vs 3 from a one-off search).
   Keep.

Verdict: change the rationale; keep the label.

  the comparison as a finding. All five own-pace judgments now read the same way: the

  the dismissals read as the author arguing with the reader; the proposals keep each
  uncertainty as what it means for the level or the outlook and drop the reassurance.

- **False precision.** Rockets "-0.04%/yr" (for a series that did not move), fusion "$3.51"

- **Levels the reader cannot picture.** "Eleven deployments" (what is one?), "1.79 h" (of

## rockets
