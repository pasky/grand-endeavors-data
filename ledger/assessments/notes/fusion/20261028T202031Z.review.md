## MAJOR findings

### 1. challenge:aneutronic-fusion — decay-time ratio presented as a reaction-rate gain

The advance for `2026-02-06-tae-nifs-lhd-icrf-pb11-rate` adds a numerical claim of approximately **1.4× rate gain**. The ledger reports alpha-count-rate **decay times** after beam switch-off: 27.2 ms with ICRF versus 19.8 ms without. Their ratio is 1.3737, establishing a roughly 37% longer decay time, not a measured 37% increase in reaction rate. The source record supports an increased alpha yield qualitatively but supplies no reaction-rate ratio. Even for exponential signals, their rate ratio depends on elapsed time and initial amplitudes; it is not the ratio of decay constants.

This is a wrong-quantity numerical inference, not merely rounding. The earlier assessment described the decay-time ratio in its change note; the staged advance now explicitly attaches that ratio to reaction rate.

**Fix:** remove the numerical rate-gain claim. State the two decay times, or the approximately 37% longer signal decay, and describe the reported yield improvement without quantifying its magnitude. If retaining a numerical reaction-rate gain, supply a source measurement of that quantity. Yellow **Inching** remains defensible after this fix; no colour change is requested.
