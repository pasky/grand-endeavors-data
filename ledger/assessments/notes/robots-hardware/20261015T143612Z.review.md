### 1. `milestone:the-wozniak-test` — incomplete release count is presented as the measured campaign rate

The input register (`eta-notes.md:88`) and `calc` call **3.5 releases/year** measured: five unseen-home evaluation releases over 17 months, subsequently multiplied by an assumed qualifying share of 0.5. The effective ledger includes another release inside that window: **`2025-11-18-pistar06-espresso-13h` reports more than two uninterrupted hours folding novel laundry in a new home**, in addition to its espresso result. The notes retain only the espresso result in their data table and omit the unseen-home result from the campaign count. No inclusion rule explains why this model release is excluded while Sunday's ACT-1 and ACT-2 releases count. Neither lack of coffee testing nor lack of a disclosed full-task success rate explains the selection consistently.

`2026-09-27-homebody-gpt6-astra-unseen-kitchen` is another potential member of this broad release class by the assessment cutoff. Its unknown success rate is correctly acknowledged, but ACT-1 also has no disclosed task success rate. HomeBody's exclusion needs a stated distinction rather than silently treating the selected five as the measured release process.

This affects the forecast, not merely a caption. Keeping the notes' 17-month window and 0.5 qualifying share, **six releases give 4.235/year and c=2.118/year**. The unchanged script then gives **2026.99 / 2027.90 / 2032.97**, versus **2027.04 / 2028.04 / 2032.97**: both p10 and p50 change calendar year. P(by end-2026) changes **0.099 → 0.115**; p_achieved remains **0.970**. Including seven releases gives c=2.471/year and p50 **2027.84**. These are diagnostics, not a prescription to adopt a new judgment without checking the class.

**Fix:** enumerate the release class against all effective events, including π*0.6's new-home result, and justify any exclusions consistently. Recompute its measured rate; separately label any selection/transfer from that rate to future qualifying coffee campaigns as assumed. If the corrected enumeration changes the stored years, attribute the change as an inherited input-count error/audit correction under rule 7, then refresh the anchor comparison. Do not silently compensate by changing the qualifying share.

### 2. `milestone:the-wozniak-test` — reference-class argument restores a specifically removed setting claim

The cross-check (`eta-notes.md:111–113`) treats Figure's January 2024 coffee demonstration as occurring in its own setting and uses it to establish a **roughly 2.7-year single-setting-to-unseen-home transition**. The effective record **`2024-01-07-figure-01-keurig-coffee`** explicitly records that verification removed the fixed-lab/not-unseen-home characterization because **neither cited source describes the setting**. Citing the effective ID does not permit reinstating its rejected characterization in the reasoning.

The ledger does establish a designed single setting for **`2025-03-19-edinburgh-ellmer-coffee`**. That precursor is about **1.5 years**, not 2.7 years, before Helix 2.5's September 2026 unseen-home campaign. The current reference-class support therefore overstates what is established about its starting point.

**Fix:** describe Figure's setting as unspecified, and either make the 2.7-year comparison explicitly only a chronology of reported demonstrations, or use ELLMER for the evidenced designed-setting comparison and recompute that interval. Reassess the cross-check without the removed claim; this does not by itself require changing the executable model or median.
